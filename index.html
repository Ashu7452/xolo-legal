Community Guidelines

XOLO · Community standards

XOLO is an adult dating and social-connection community for gay men. These Guidelines apply to profiles, messages, groups, media and all other areas of the Service.

1. Respect and Consent

  • Treat members with dignity and respect.
  • Respect personal boundaries and consent, including when someone declines contact.
  • Do not harass, threaten, intimidate, bully or repeatedly contact someone who asks you to stop.
  • Do not discriminate against or abuse people based on identity or personal characteristics.

2. Prohibited Content and Behaviour

  • Any sexual, exploitative or inappropriate content involving a minor, or attempts to contact or solicit a minor.
  • Non-consensual intimate images, sexual exploitation or threats to publish intimate content.
  • Blackmail, extortion, scams, impersonation or deceptive financial requests.
  • Threats, hate speech, stalking, targeted harassment or encouragement of violence.
  • Spam, fake profiles, malicious links or attempts to obtain passwords, OTPs or financial credentials.
  • Sharing another person's private or identifying information without permission.
  • Content or conduct that violates applicable law or others' rights.

3. Authentic Profiles

Use a profile that represents you honestly. Do not misleadingly use another person's photographs or identity. Do not create deceptive accounts to evade restrictions or manipulate members.

4. Groups and Conversations

Respect group rules and moderator decisions. Do not add people in ways that disregard their consent. Do not redistribute private conversations, group content or personal media outside the intended audience without permission.

5. Reporting and Enforcement

Report concerning accounts or content through in-app tools where available, or contact support. Reports may be reviewed and proportionate action may be taken, including content removal, feature restrictions, suspension or account termination.

6. Stay Safe

Be cautious with links, requests for money and requests for verification codes. Do not send money solely because someone asks through chat. Consider meeting in a public place and tell someone you trust about your plans.
