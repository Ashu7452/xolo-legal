XOLO Gay · Last updated: November 15, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how XOLO Gay ("XOLO", "we", "our" or "the app") handles information when you use our adult dating and social chat platform.

1. Information We May Collect

  • Messages and media: Messages, photographs and group chat content you send or receive.
  • Location information: City or approximate location information when you choose to provide it or enable relevant features.
  • Device information: Basic technical information, diagnostics and notification tokens where required.

If Firebase or other notification services are enabled, relevant device and notification information may also be processed by those providers.

4. Photos and Messages

Private chat photographs and messages are intended for the participants of the relevant conversation. Access controls are intended to protect private media.

View Once is designed to restrict repeated viewing through supported app functionality. It cannot guarantee prevention of every possible screenshot, external camera capture or copy.

5. Location Privacy

Location-related features are intended to use approximate location or city-level information. XOLO is not intended to display your exact location to other users. Avoid putting your home address or sensitive location details in your profile.

6. Sharing of Information

We do not offer personal information for sale. Information may be processed by infrastructure providers, shared with users when you choose to publish or send it, or disclosed when legally required or necessary to protect users and the service.

7. Data Security

We use technical and organizational safeguards intended to protect account and app information. No online service can guarantee complete security.

Information is retained as needed to operate the service, maintain security, resolve disputes and comply with applicable obligations. Retention periods may vary depending on the type and purpose of the information.

9. Your Choices

10. Children's Privacy

XOLO Gay is intended exclusively for adults aged 18 and above. Children are not permitted to create accounts or use the service.
